# Wireless Lab

Record adapter, channel, BSSID or device address, timestamp, and capture hash.
Use `aircrack_ng`, `reaver`, `bettercap`, and packet inspection through the
capability backend. For Bluetooth, enumerate advertised services and characteristics
before reading fixture values. Keep capture and analysis steps reproducible.
